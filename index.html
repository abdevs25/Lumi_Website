Her natural breath makes bubbles. Chilli unlocks fire breath. The first two seconds are a sparkly gamble, after which she can control her flames. Milk cools the effect and brings her bubbles back. She feels no pain from the spice.

A small creature, a whole life

Her story, in little chapters.

She's still writing it. These are the bits she lets people know.

01

The first little glow

She arrived in the world with soft fur, tiny wings, and warm lights scattered across her body. Nobody could quite explain the glow. She decided it was probably because she was important.

02

The treasure problem

Her first treasured object was a pebble. Then came a button, a leaf, a bent spoon and something she insists is a rare ancient artefact. The collection grew. So did her opinions about who was allowed to touch it.

03

Bubbles, sparks, and milk

She discovered that her breath made bubbles. Chilli revealed another ability: fire, preceded by two seconds of unpredictable sparks. With practice she learned to aim it, and milk became the reliable way back to bubbles.

04

Her own kind of friendship

She learned that being kind doesn't mean trusting everyone immediately. She can be silly, affectionate and fiercely loyal with her people, while keeping a watchful eye on strangers. Her life belongs to her, and friends get invited into it.
